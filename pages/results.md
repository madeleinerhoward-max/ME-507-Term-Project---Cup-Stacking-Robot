@page results_page Performance / Results

For our final project, the robot was able to successfully complete repeatable
homing operations and demonstrated reliable motion in all three axes. The
vacuum and suction cup pickup system was capable of lifting and transporting
individual cups within the printer bed. During testing, the robot
consistently returned to its reference positions using the installed limit
switches, which improved repeatability and reduced accumulated positioning
errors. Although the final design achieved its primary motion-control
objectives, additional tuning would further improve the cup stacking speed
and placement accuracy. Our team initially wanted to add an IR sensor and
additional bump sensor to the suction cup pickup system to give additional
detail on where the cups were located and when a cup had been fully picked
up, but we ran out of time on the project.
