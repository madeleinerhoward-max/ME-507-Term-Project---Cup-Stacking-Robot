@page lessons_page Lessons Learned

One of the most valuable lessons from this project was the importance of
system-level integration. The mechanical, electrical, and software subsystems
functioned independently, but we had to do additional work to ensure that
they operated reliably together. Power-distribution issues, current surges,
and hardware debugging showed the team how important it was to consider the
interactions between each of the subsystems early in our design process. The
project also provided practical experience in PCB design, embedded firmware
development, motion control, and robotic system integration. For any future
iterations of our design, we would consider adding additional current
monitoring, improved protection circuitry, and further automation of the
cup-stacking process.
