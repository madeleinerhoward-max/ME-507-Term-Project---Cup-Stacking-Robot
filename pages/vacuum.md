@page vacuum_page Vacuum Motor Control

Our vacuum pickup system is controlled through PWM generated by Timer 1 on
the STM32. Rather than immediately applying full power to the vacuum motor, a
software-controlled ramp function gradually increases the PWM duty cycle over
a specified time interval. We added the soft start to reduce current surges
and minimize sudden load changes on our power distribution system. The same
ramping function is also used when shutting down the vacuum motor, resulting
in smoother operation and reduced electrical stress on the system components.

See @ref vacuum_ramp() for the implementation.
