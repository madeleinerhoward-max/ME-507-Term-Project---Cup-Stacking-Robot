@page mechanical_page Mechanical Design

As mentioned before, the mechanical structure of the robot was based on a
modified Ender-series 3D printer frame. Reusing the printer provided
numerous advantages for our team, including an already assembled and rigid
aluminum extrusion frame, integrated linear motion systems, stepper motors,
limit switches for the axes, and existing belt-drive mechanisms. By using
the existing gantry system, our team was able to focus more time on
developing custom control electronics and our suction cup integration rather
than designing a gantry system from scratch.

The robot operates using three axes of motion. The X and Y axes position the
suction cup horizontally across the bed, while the Z axis provides vertical
movement. A vacuum-based pickup mechanism was mounted to one of the sides of
the aluminum extrusion frame using team-designed and 3D-printed mounts. Our
team used CAD to verify component interactions, mounting locations, and
overall system fit. The final design also incorporated a dedicated
electronics compartment within the printer frame for housing the custom PCB
and the AC-to-DC switcher.
