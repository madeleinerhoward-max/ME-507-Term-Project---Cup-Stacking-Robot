Pick-and-stack coordinates

Edit these. All positions are in mm from home (0,0,0). Z is the bed: 0 = bottom (the cranked datum), positive raises the bed. The suction cup is at a fixed height on the X carriage; the bed lifts a cup up to it (vacuum on), lowers leaving the cup held, traverses, lifts the held cup onto the target cup, releases (vacuum off), and lowers.
